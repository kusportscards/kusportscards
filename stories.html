Story images

Make a Story image for every card at once and save them all to your phone. This page isn't linked from your site.

Then in Instagram
  1. Start a new Story and open your camera roll.
  2. Tap Select (the stacked squares) and tap up to 10 images, in the order you want them.
  3. Tap Next and share. Each card posts as its own slide. Repeat for the next 10.
